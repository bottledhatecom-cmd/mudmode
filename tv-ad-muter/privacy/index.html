TV ad muter: Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026. TV ad muter is made by Hestertainment LLC, Illinois, USA. Contact for privacy questions and requests: reports@mudmodecrew.com.

This policy covers only the TV ad muter Android app. This web page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives standard request data such as your IP address when you open it; the mudmodecrew.com website, including this page, is covered by its own privacy notice at https://mudmodecrew.com/privacy.html.

What the app uses.

The microphone, only after you tap Start, with a "Listening" notification until you tap Stop. The app turns the TV sound into numbers on your phone to guess when ads are on. That sound is not kept.

What stays on your phone.

If you turn on "Record for tuning", the app saves room sound, your break marks and per-second sound numbers in its own private folder. The sound recordings are never sent. The app has no internet access and sends nothing by itself; numbers leave only if you send them (below). Your recordings and their numbers are excluded from cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer, and stay on your phone until you delete them ("Delete" next to a recording, or "Delete all recordings") or uninstall the app.

What you can choose to send us.

"Send my tuning data" makes a file of numbers only (no audio): your break marks and answers, one row of sound numbers per second of each recording you include, fingerprints of ad breaks you confirmed, the recording dates and times, the app version and a random tester code made on your phone. You see what's in it and send it yourself with an app you pick (for example email); that app's own terms apply to it. This data can show when your TV was on and which ads played. If you email it, we also receive your email address.

How we use it.

Only to test and improve the app's ad detection. We don't sell it, share it, use it for ads, or combine it with other data. We make no voiceprints and don't identify voices.

How we keep it safe.

Files are kept only in the developer's email account and computer, protected by passwords and 2-step verification, and seen only by the developer. Email to the contact address passes through a forwarding service (ImprovMX), which does not keep message contents after delivery.

How long we keep it, and deleting it.

Until tuning is done, and no more than 180 days after we receive it. Fingerprints of TV ads themselves may be kept after your code and times are removed. To have your files deleted sooner, email us; we delete them within 7 days.

Children.

The app is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes.

We'll update this page and its date, and tell testers before a change that sends new kinds of data.
