How we count: the meter splits the text into words, numbers, symbols and line breaks, and counts about 1 token per short word, more for long words, and less than 1 per symbol. That's a rule, not a real tokenizer. We checked it on Nov 4, 2026 against one real tokenizer (OpenAI's o200k) on 121 samples we hadn't tuned it on: on English text it was off by 4% on average (8 in 10 samples within 5% low to 6% high), and on code by 7% on average (8 in 10 within 8% low to 14% high). Claude, Grok and Gemini use their own tokenizers, so their counts can differ more, and it can be far off for non-English text and emoji. Each AI reply is counted as reading the whole chat before it, so input grows as the chat goes on. Very long chats can cost more per token. Every number here is an estimate.
