# mudmode kit: out now

The full mudmode kit is **out now**: see [pricing on mudmodecrew.com](https://mudmodecrew.com/#w-price). The free kit is in [starter-kit/](../starter-kit/).

## What it gets you
- One rulebook your AI assistants share, even when they come from different AI makers
- Paying for an AI? mudmode helps you get the most out of it: one page of rules you own, and handoffs so the next chat picks up where the last one stopped
- Pick up where you left off, in any new chat
- Rules that ask your AI to check its work and show you the evidence before it says "done"
- A lessons book you can turn on: when something goes wrong, the crew writes it down and reads it before the next job
- Shared lessons (just starting): members can post a short lesson by hand; approved lessons are planned for future kit releases, and none has shipped yet (the screening step for member lessons is still being built). Always opt-in and 18+; your post shows your GitHub username
- Rules that tell your AIs to wait for your yes before anything risky
- Works on any ordinary computer, even a laptop with no graphics card. Got a strong PC? You can let mudmode use it. No strong PC? Everything still works.
