# MODULES-ON (mudmode): full mode, no module file loads

Mode: full. `CORE.md` is the combined core: the 25 rules of mudmode 1.2.3, word for word (copy: `cores/CORE-FULL.md`). Also on: "mudmode report" = the daily report from `templates/DAILY-REPORT.md` into `reports/REPORT-<date>.md`. "mudmode lite" or a module: `modules/README.md` (backup first; nothing is deleted). Short name (the full rule, also in `cores/CORE-LITE.md` under "Short name"): "mud" stands for "mudmode" only when it is at the start of the owner's message and the whole message is exactly "mud" plus one word from this closed list: status, report, modules, full, lite, quiet. Any other message with "mud" in it is ordinary words. "mud status", "mud report" and "mud modules" work at once; for "mud full", "mud lite" and "mud quiet", first ask in one line and act only on a clear yes. No "mud" phrase is ever an OK for anything that needs one. If `CORE-LOCAL.md` says the short name is off, or "mud" already means something else here, that wins; say once, in one plain line, to use "mudmode".
