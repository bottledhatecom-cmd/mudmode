# MODULES-ON (mudmode): full mode, no module file loads

Mode: full. `CORE.md` is the combined core: the 25 rules of mudmode 1.2.3, word for word (copy: `cores/CORE-FULL.md`). Also on: "mudmode report" = the daily report from `templates/DAILY-REPORT.md` into `reports/REPORT-<date>.md`. "mudmode lite" or a module: `modules/README.md` (backup first; nothing is deleted).
