# What to say

There are no buttons or codes. You type plain words to your AI. Here are the ones that get things done in the mudmode free Crew kit (mudgo words match the Full Kit).

## To set up (once)

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| `Read mudmode-chat-setup.md and set me up.` | In a chat app with Projects (add `FOR-CHAT-APPS/mudmode-chat-setup.md` to the project's files first): your AI sets mudmode up and asks a few short questions. |
| `Read README.md and set me up.` | In an AI that works with files on your computer (open it in this free kit folder first): the same setup, and it asks before it runs anything. |
| `defaults` | During setup, when your AI lists its questions with a suggested answer for each: use all the suggested answers. |

ChatGPT: make a Project, add `FOR-CHAT-APPS/mudmode-chat-setup.md` to its files, and say the same words. (Projects are in ChatGPT's sidebar, on free plans too.) Your AI tells you where to paste the short settings block it gives you.

## Every day

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| `mudmode` | Your AI answers "Woot! mudmode is on." and asks what you need. |
| `mudhelp` | A short list of the words you can say, one line each (this page, in short). |
| `mudmode status` | A one-line verdict, then what's open, what needs you, and the next step. |
| `handoff` | Before you stop: your AI writes a note so the next chat picks up where this one stopped. In a chat app, paste the block it gives you as the first message of your next chat. |
| `mudmode report` | The daily report: what mudmode is doing, what it learned, and what you chose to share with other members. |
| Your OK, naming the one thing | Approves that one action, one time. A broad "do it all" never covers a deploy, a store upload, sending, buying or deleting. |

## Change how it works

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| `mudgo on` / `mudgo off` / `mudgo auto` | mudmode works everyday-first: plain words, and every safety rule on. mudgo adds the rules for technical work (putting a site live, branches, reviews, permission files). `auto` (the start) turns it on by itself when the work needs it and tells you; `mudgo on` keeps it on; `mudgo off` keeps it off. The safety rules stay on either way, and nothing is deleted. Older words still work: `pro mode`, `pro off`, `pro auto`, `everyday mode`, `mudmode full`, `mudmode lite`. |
| `change my mudmode settings` | Change your name, time zone, what your AI must always ask about first, or your word rules. |

Short name: `mud` works too, as the whole message: `mud status`, `mud report`, `mud modules`, and `mud go`, `mud everyday` or `mud quiet` (for those three your AI asks you to confirm first). `mudmode` always works. A "mud" message is never an OK for anything. (If your computer already has a `mud` command, setup leaves it alone and you keep saying `mudmode`.)

Want more? Say `mudmode modules` to see the extra pieces and which ones are on. (With mudgo on they are all on already.)

Anything else, just ask in plain words, like "help me plan my week."
