# What to say

There are no buttons or codes. You type plain words to your AI. Here are the ones that get things done in the free mudmode Crew kit.

## To set up (once)

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| `Read mudmode-chat-lite.md and set me up.` | In a chat app with Projects (add `FOR-CHAT-APPS/mudmode-chat-lite.md` to the project's files first): your AI sets mudmode up and asks a few short questions. |
| `Read README.md and set me up.` | In an AI that works with files on your computer (open it in this folder first): the same setup, and it asks before it runs anything. |
| `defaults` | During setup, when your AI lists its questions with a suggested answer for each: use all the suggested answers. |

## Every day

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| `mudmode` | Your AI answers "Woot! mudmode is on." and asks what you need. |
| `mudhelp` | A short list of the words you can say, one line each (this page, in short). |
| `mudmode status` | A one-line verdict, then what's open, what needs you, and the next step. |
| `handoff` | Before you stop: your AI writes a note so the next chat picks up where this one stopped. In a chat app, paste the block it gives you as the first message of your next chat. |
| `mudmode report` | The daily report: what mudmode is doing, what it learned, and what you chose to share with other members. |
| Your OK, naming the one thing | Approves that one action, one time. A broad "do it all" never covers a deploy, a store upload, sending, buying or deleting. |

## Change how it works

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| `mudmode full` / `mudmode lite` | Full turns every rule on (all 25 rules in one rule book). Lite goes back to the one-page safety core plus any modules you turned on. The safety rules stay on either way, and nothing is deleted. In a chat app, your AI tells you which file to put in your project instead. |
| `change my mudmode settings` | Change your name, time zone, what your AI must always ask about first, or your word rules. |

Short name: `mud` works too, as the whole message: `mud status`, `mud report`, `mud modules`, and `mud full`, `mud lite` or `mud quiet` (for those three your AI asks you to confirm first). `mudmode` always works. A "mud" message is never an OK for anything. (If your computer already has a `mud` command, setup leaves it alone and you keep saying `mudmode`.)

Want more? In lite mode (the free kit's default), say `mudmode modules` to see the extra pieces and which ones are on.

Anything else, just ask in plain words, like "help me plan my week."
