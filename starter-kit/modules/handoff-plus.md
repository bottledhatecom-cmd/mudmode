# mudmode module: handoff-plus

**Adds:** the full 11-line handoff, the last 4 handoffs in `handoff/` (older ones in `handoff/archive/`), and short-answer mode for long chats. Its rules replace the *everyday* lines in `CORE.md` (the same words are in `CORE.md` Part 2, mudgo).

22. Handoffs bring over everything (see `templates/HANDOFF.md`). Keep the last 4 handoffs active, archive the rest. When a chat is about 75% full, switch to short answers and offer a handoff.

**Files:** `templates/HANDOFF.md` (the full form; everyday use takes `templates/HANDOFF-LITE.md`).
**More:** the Full Kit book `full-kit/MUDMODE.md` §10.
