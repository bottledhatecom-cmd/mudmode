To the extent your lesson has no copyright (for example, text your AI wrote), you agree we may use it on the same terms. This license doesn't depend on you getting a reward.
  3. What you must not send. No passwords, keys, tokens or other secrets. No personal data about you or anyone else (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, account names, health or family details). No employer or client confidential information. No text you copied from somewhere else that you don't have the right to share. If your AI wrote the draft, check it before you send it.

  6. Credit is up to you. Choose "credit me by my handle" or "keep me anonymous" when you send. Anonymous is the default. Even if you ask for credit, we may leave credit out where it doesn't fit (for example a short digest line), and lessons merged from several members may be credited as "from the hive". To the extent the law allows, you waive, and agree not to assert, any "moral rights" in your lesson (such as a right to be named or to object to edits) against uses these terms allow.

Those rewards are the only thing owed for a lesson. Rewards have no cash value, and we can change them for future lessons. How you agree

Put this line at the top of the lesson you send. Your AI may help draft the lesson, but only you decide to agree and send it:

I agree to the mudmode lesson terms (v2) and I'm 18 or older.

Then add Keep me anonymous (the default) or Credit me as <your handle> on the next line. A lesson without the agreement line isn't used. If these terms change, the version number changes and we'll ask again on your next lesson; lessons you already sent stay under the version you agreed to.
