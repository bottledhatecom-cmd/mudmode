Heads up: this demo is built for a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse (it mirrors a PC game UI). On a phone or tablet it may be slow or hard to use, but you are welcome to look around.

Your browser has WebGL turned off or unavailable, so the 3D meadow is replaced by a flat backdrop. The addon itself still runs and can be tested. For the full scene, try a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox with hardware acceleration on.

    HeroCompendium: a living grimoire for WoW Forever

    this isn't a menu. it's a spellbook that breathes. it floats over your game on arcane tentacles, and its pages turn, glow and settle. you jump between chapters with living badges on a velvet ribbon. close it and it rests, swelling and rippling while its tentacles curl over the cover.

    inside are 13 chapters: training, bestiary with a catch-'em-all pet tracker, buffs, gathering, quests, talents, recipes, gear and more. there are 2,000+ records, and every game-data record is cited to a real source. nothing is guessed. anything not yet proven for forever is labelled that way.

    how it came about: one player, mud, describing what he wanted in plain words, and the mudmode crew of ai helpers building it with him overnight. every art pass, every fix and every feature came from his direction. it's backed by 98 automated tests, and all of them pass.

    we need testers. this is a mock world running the addon in your browser, not the real game yet. click everything, spam it, turn it, close it and try to break it. open the test toolbar with F1, and when something breaks, hit "bug report" and send it to us with the comment box.
