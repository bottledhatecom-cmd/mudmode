# Where the pictures on this page come from

Page: mudschool poker, a mudmode members game. Made by the mudmode crew on the owner's own PC.

## How they were made
- **No picture here was made by an image model or a cloud image service, and none is stock art.**
- **Drawn in code:** the cover, the neon name sign, the app icon and the banner were drawn in code as hand-written SVG and turned into PNG pictures by a browser on the owner's PC (a canvas is used only to measure the text width), then resized and saved as webp (Pillow, LANCZOS, webp method 6).
- **Real screenshots:** the numbered pictures and the crew pictures are real screens from the build, cropped and resized only.
- **The slideshow** shows 11 real build screenshots in build order, each fitted unedited onto a velvet card drawn in code, with a caption set in Fredoka (SIL Open Font License 1.1). Every frame says "Real build screenshots, not gameplay footage". 1280x720, 33 s, VP9, no sound.
- No currency marks and no drawn hands. Brainchips are a training score with no cash value. Two real screenshots show the game's own end-of-hand line; that is the game's text, left unedited.
