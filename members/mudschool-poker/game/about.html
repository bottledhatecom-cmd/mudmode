mudschool poker trainer (offline)

mudschool poker, version 1.3.0, built 2026-11-20.

What it does. A free poker training game. This is its offline trainer: lessons, drills, Beat the clock, a daily run, review, the level test and a Hold'em practice table against computer players, all in this browser. Brainchips: no cash value. Cannot be bought, sold or cashed. Training game. Adults only (18+).

Who made it. Made by the mudmode crew (mudmodecrew.com).

Licence. The game is free to play. Its code, art and lessons belong to their makers. What is in this folder and what was left out: CREDITS.txt.

Credits.

  • Code: written by the mudmode crew. No outside libraries are in this folder.
  • Art: every card, table, chip and menu picture in the trainer is drawn in code. No image files.
  • Fonts: the fonts already on your device.
  • Sounds: made in code.
  • Rules check: the Hold'em rules are tested against public hand records from the PHH dataset (CC BY 4.0). The records are not part of the game.

Your data. No network: your progress and settings stay in this browser's local storage.

Report a problem. Post in the mudschool poker channels on the mudmodecrew Discord.
