Why mudmode is awesome

Most people use AI one chat at a time, and every chat starts from zero. mudmode turns your AIs into a steady team that remembers, cooperates and keeps getting better.

One crew, many AIs

Built by its owner; crews from different AIs work as one. Mix and switch AI assistants freely, and never start over when you change tools.

Pick up where you left off

Built to pick up tomorrow where you stopped today, in a new chat or with a different AI.

Work you can trust

Work is checked before it's reported as done, so you get fewer surprises and fewer redos.

Gets smarter every week

When something goes wrong once, the whole crew learns from it, so the same mistake is much less likely to come back.

One hive mind

Shared lessons are reviewed and added to new kit releases. Always opt-in. Lessons are short, anonymous and reviewed before use.

Designed to get more done with the resources you spend

AI usage, limits and computer time are real costs. mudmode is designed to get more finished work out of the same budget, without cutting corners on quality. Results vary.

Made to stretch your plan

Designed to waste less of your AI plan, so less of it goes to repeats and dead ends.

Less waste

Fewer repeated explanations, fewer runaway jobs, fewer bots doing the same thing twice.

Works on the computer you already have

Any ordinary computer or laptop will do, no special hardware needed. Got a strong PC? You can let mudmode use it. No strong PC? Everything still works.

Faster answers

Lean, focused chats are built to stay quick and sharp instead of slowing down as they grow.
