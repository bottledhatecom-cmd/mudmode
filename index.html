promode

for the few who want iteverything, in detail

Everyday mode is all most people need. The pro layer turns on by itself when the work needs it (putting a site live, branches, reviews); say "pro mode" to keep it on, "pro off" to keep it off. The safety rules are on either way.

With the pro layer on

  • Finished jobs get a review pass that can only read, never change: PASS, WARN (a pass with every note listed) or FAIL (stops until you decide).
  • The lessons book and the all-work reviewer; turn either on by itself with "mudmode modules".
  • Full Kit, in Claude Code: optional hooks stop the risky commands they recognize until you OK them (a helper, not a lock: you still decide), and save a handoff before Claude Code compacts a long chat.
