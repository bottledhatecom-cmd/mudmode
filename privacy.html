Privacy note

Updated October 21, 2026

Who we are

mudmodecrew.com is run by Hestertainment LLC, USA. Contact (including privacy requests): bottledhate.com [at] gmail [dot] com. General questions about mudmode can also go to GitHub Discussions.

This website

Static pages with no analytics, no ads, no cookies and no external fonts. We collect nothing ourselves. Like every website, our host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.) receives standard request data such as your IP address and browser type; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Members chat

Only if you click "Open the members chat", your browser loads giscus (giscus.app) and GitHub. To post you sign in with GitHub; giscus keeps a sign-in token in your browser's local storage. Posts are public on GitHub and are governed by GitHub's Terms and Privacy Statement, including GitHub's own uses of public content. Posting is covered by the mudmode contributor terms.

Lessons you choose to share

If you post a lesson, we may copy its text and your GitHub username to our private files to review it. We use it only to improve mudmode. We delete the raw copy within 30 days of review and keep only the edited, anonymous lesson. We never sell personal data or use it for ads.

Purchases

Nothing is sold on this site today. If that changes, this note will be updated before any sale.

Your choices

You can delete your own GitHub posts at any time. Contact us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you; we reply within 30 days. If you are in the EU/UK you may also complain to your data protection authority. Our legal bases are your consent (lessons) and our legitimate interest in running the community.

Changes

We will update this note and its date when anything changes.
