Privacy note

Updated October 22, 2026

Who we are

mudmodecrew.com is run by Hestertainment LLC, an Illinois limited liability company (USA), which also sells and licenses mudmode kits and is responsible for the personal data described in this note (the data controller). Contact (including privacy requests): bottledhate.com [at] gmail [dot] com. General questions about mudmode can also go to GitHub Discussions.

This website

Static pages with no analytics, no ads, no cookies and no external fonts. We collect nothing ourselves. Like every website, our host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.) receives standard request data such as your IP address and browser type; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Members chat

Only if you click "Open the members chat", your browser loads giscus (giscus.app) and GitHub. To post you sign in with GitHub; giscus keeps a sign-in token in your browser's local storage. Posts are public on GitHub and are governed by GitHub's Terms and Privacy Statement, including GitHub's own uses of public content. Posting is covered by the mudmode contributor terms.

Lessons you choose to share

If you post a lesson, we may copy its text and your GitHub username to our private files to review it. We use it only to improve mudmode. We delete the raw copy within 30 days of review and keep only the edited lesson (anonymous unless you asked for credit by your handle), plus a record of your agreement to the mudmode lesson terms. You must be 18 or older to send a lesson. We never sell personal data or use it for ads.

Purchases

mudmode kits, paid and free, are sold and delivered through Gumroad, not on this site. Gumroad (Gumroad, Inc.) is the seller of record: it takes your payment, handles sales tax, sends your receipt and delivers your files and updates to your Gumroad library. When you buy or download a kit, Gumroad receives your email address, your name if you give one, your payment details and standard request data such as your IP address. Payment details are handled by Gumroad and its payment processors; we never see your full card number. See Gumroad's Privacy Policy.

From Gumroad we receive your purchase email, your name if you gave one, what you got and when, the price, your license key, that you accepted the license, any refund or dispute, your country (Gumroad estimates it from your IP address, sometimes with a state or ZIP code), the site that sent you to the product, and whether you agreed to update emails. We use it only to deliver your kit and updates, check licenses, handle refunds and support, and keep tax records. We may use service providers, such as email and AI tools, to help with support and bookkeeping; they may only use your data to help us, and we only use AI tools under settings or terms that keep your data out of model training. We keep purchase records while your license and updates are active, and up to 7 years after your last purchase for tax and accounting, then delete them. We never sell it or use it for ads.

We add no trackers: we turn off Gumroad's third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel) for our products, and our links to Gumroad carry no tracking codes. Gumroad's own pages and emails use Gumroad's cookies and analytics, under its policy. You can turn off our emails in Gumroad at any time. Your kit download stays in your Gumroad library (or your receipt's download link); if you turn emails off and miss an update pack, contact us and we'll send it. Our website sets no tracking, so there's nothing for Do Not Track signals to switch off; Gumroad's pages follow Gumroad's policy. Who handles your purchase data: Hestertainment LLC (Illinois) sells and licenses mudmode kits and is responsible for your purchase data. mudmode is created by Michael J Hester, who owns the copyright. Our Gumroad store is held by mudmode's authorized distributor, who lists mudmode kits on Gumroad for Hestertainment LLC and may use your purchase data only on our behalf and only as this note says. Questions or requests: see our contact details.

Your choices

You can delete your own GitHub posts at any time. Contact us to see, correct, delete, move (export) or object to our use of what we hold about you; we reply within 30 days. Gumroad handles requests about its own records; see Gumroad's Privacy Policy. If you are in the EU/UK you may also complain to your data protection authority. Our legal bases are your consent (lessons), our contract with you (purchases and lesson terms), our legal duties (tax records) and our legitimate interest in running the community.

We will update this note and its date when anything changes.
