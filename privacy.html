Privacy note

Updated October 31, 2026

Who we are

mudmodecrew.com is run by Hestertainment LLC, an Illinois limited liability company (USA), which also sells and licenses mudmode kits and is responsible for the personal data described in this note (the data controller). Contact (including privacy requests): bottledhate.com [at] gmail [dot] com. General questions about mudmode can also go to GitHub Discussions.

This website

Static pages with no analytics, no ads, no cookies of our own and no external fonts. We collect nothing ourselves, except for the member sign-in check described below. The Full Kit button on our home page and Start here page opens Gumroad's product page in a pop-up over our page, or in this tab if the pop-up can't open or load; see Purchases. Like every website, our host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.) receives standard request data such as your IP address and browser type; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. Downloading the free kit is an ordinary request to our host. The start buttons on the home page run only in your browser: when the page loads, they load the two prompt files from this site; they put a prompt on your clipboard only when you tap one; and they store nothing (no cookies, no local storage). Which button you tap is never sent to us. The Start here page remembers which step you’re on and which AI you picked, in this browser’s local storage only; it doesn’t send them to us. Its “Forget my choices” button, or clearing this site’s data in your browser, removes them. If you follow a link to ChatGPT, Claude or Grok, that site’s own terms and privacy policy apply; our links send no referrer, no tracking codes and no prompt.

mudpity saves your best score, graphics quality and sound setting in your browser's local storage on your own device. None of it is sent to us.

Members chat

Only if you click "Open the members chat", your browser loads giscus (giscus.app) and GitHub. To post you sign in with GitHub; giscus keeps a sign-in token in your browser's local storage. Posts are public on GitHub and are governed by GitHub's Terms and Privacy Statement, including GitHub's own uses of public content. Posting is covered by the mudmode contributor terms.

Member sign-in

If you sign in on the members page, your browser sends the license key you paste to our sign-in checker, a small program we run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) at mudmode-members.bottledhate-com.workers.dev. Like any web service, Cloudflare receives standard request data such as your IP address and browser type; see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. The checker sends your key to Gumroad's license check to confirm that your purchase is in good standing (not refunded, charged back, disputed or ended); this does not count as a use of your key. Gumroad's answer includes details of your purchase, such as your email; the checker uses only whether the purchase is good and which kit it is, and passes nothing else on. The checker does not save your key or Gumroad's answer. To slow down repeated guessing, it counts sign-in attempts per IP address and per key over one-minute windows (the key is first turned into a one-way code, a hash); Cloudflare holds those counts. We have turned off the checker's request logs. After a good check, this browser tab keeps a signed sign-in token in its session storage (not a cookie). The token holds only which kit you have and when it expires. It stops working after one hour, and it is removed when you sign out or close the tab. Sign-in sets no cookies.

Lessons you choose to share

If you post a lesson, we may copy its text and your GitHub username to our private files to review it. We use it only to improve mudmode. We delete the raw copy within 30 days of review and keep only the edited lesson (uncredited unless you asked for credit by your handle), plus a record of your agreement to the mudmode lesson terms. You must be 18 or older to send a lesson. We never sell personal data or use it for ads.

Purchases

The free Crew kit and the free Windows installer (mudmode one-button) download straight from this site: no email, no form, no account. The paid Full Kit (and optional tips) are sold and delivered through Gumroad, not on this site; if you got the free kit through Gumroad earlier, this section applies to that copy too. Gumroad (Gumroad, Inc.) is the seller of record: it takes your payment, handles sales tax, sends your receipt and delivers your files and updates to your Gumroad library. When you buy or download a kit on Gumroad, Gumroad receives your email address, your name if you give one, your payment details and standard request data such as your IP address. Payment details are handled by Gumroad and its payment processors; we never see your full card number. See Gumroad's Privacy Policy. If you send an optional tip through Gumroad, everything in this note about purchases applies to it too.

Our home page and Start here page load nothing from Gumroad until you tap the Full Kit button. When you tap it, Gumroad's product page opens in a pop-up over our page (or in this tab, if the pop-up can't open or load), and Gumroad receives standard request data such as your IP address and browser type, plus at most the address of our page you were on, without its query or # part, so never your step or the AI you picked. Gumroad's page can set its own cookies then, including Google Analytics cookies, even inside the pop-up; whether your browser keeps them depends on its settings. That product page is Gumroad's own page, with Gumroad's cookies and analytics (including Google Analytics), under Gumroad's Privacy Policy. Paying finishes on gumroad.com. Our other pages, including the members page, load nothing from Gumroad.

From Gumroad we receive your purchase email, your name if you gave one, what you got and when, the price, your license key, that you accepted the license, any refund or dispute, your country (Gumroad estimates it from your IP address, sometimes with a state or ZIP code), the site that sent you to the product, and whether you agreed to update emails. We use it only to deliver your kit and updates, check licenses, handle refunds and support, and keep tax records. We may use service providers, such as email and AI tools, to help with support and bookkeeping; they may only use your data to help us, and we only use AI tools under settings or terms that keep your data out of model training. We keep purchase records while your license and updates are active, and up to 7 years after your last purchase for tax and accounting, then delete them. We never sell it or use it for ads.

We add no trackers: we turn off Gumroad's third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel) for our products, and our links to Gumroad carry no tracking codes of our own. When the Full Kit pop-up opens, it tells Gumroad the address of our page you were on, without its query or # part; that is how Gumroad tells us the site that sent you. If the pop-up can't open, the plain link tells Gumroad at most our site's name (mudmodecrew.com). Gumroad's own pages and emails use Gumroad's cookies and analytics, under its policy. You can turn off our emails in Gumroad at any time. Your kit download stays in your Gumroad library (or your receipt's download link); if you turn emails off and miss an update pack, contact us and we'll send it. Our website sets no tracking, so there's nothing for Do Not Track signals to switch off; Gumroad's pages follow Gumroad's policy. Who handles your purchase data: Hestertainment LLC (Illinois) sells and licenses mudmode kits and is responsible for your purchase data. mudmode is created by Michael J Hester, who owns the copyright. Our Gumroad store is held by mudmode's authorized distributor, who lists mudmode kits on Gumroad for Hestertainment LLC and may use your purchase data only on our behalf and only as this note says. Questions or requests: see our contact details.
